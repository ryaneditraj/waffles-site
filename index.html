Questions people ask

What is the Potato Fan Club?

Potato Fan Club is a small static website where you click a potato, read a random potato fact, and join a fan club in your browser. Ryan Editraj made it for the Hack Club Waffles program.

How do I run this potato website on my computer?

Clone the repository and open index.html in a browser, or start a local server with python3 -m http.server and visit http://127.0.0.1:8000.

Does joining the club save or share my name?

No. The name stays in this browser tab. It is not sent to a server, saved in a database, or shown to anyone else.

Who made the Potato Fan Club?
